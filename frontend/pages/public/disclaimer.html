Medical disclaimer

Please read this before relying on anything you see here.

In an emergency, do not use this website.Call 112 or 108 now, or go to the nearest hospital.

General information only

The articles, the "Which doctor do I need?" tool and other content on this site are meant to help you understand your options. They are not a diagnosis, and they do not replace advice from a doctor who has examined you.

The doctor finder

The finder looks for words in what you write and suggests a department to start with. It does not understand your health. It can be wrong. If you are worried, speak to a doctor.

Demo project

CuraLink Health is a learning project. The doctors, hospitals, prices, ambulance service and reviews shown here are fictional, and no ambulance is sent when you use the request form.

Mental health

If you are thinking about harming yourself, you are not alone. In India you can call Tele-MANAS on 14416 (free), or 112 in an emergency.
