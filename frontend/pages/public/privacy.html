Privacy policy

What this demo website keeps about you, and why.

This is a demonstration project. The text below explains how the demo behaves. If this were a real service, a lawyer would need to review it.

What we keep

  • Account details: your name, email, mobile number and password. The password is stored only as a one-way scrambled value, never as plain text.
  • Health details you add: your health record, uploaded reports and prescriptions.
  • Bookings and payments: appointments, tokens and demo payment records. No real card or bank details are ever collected.
  • Searches: what you search for when finding a doctor, so we can see what people look for. Guests are not identified.
  • Messages and requests: anything you send through the contact form, ambulance request, insurance form or video room chat.

Who can see it

  • You can see all of your own information.
  • A doctor can see your health record and reports only when you have a confirmed or completed appointment with them. Each time is recorded in an audit log.
  • Administrators manage accounts, bookings and requests. They cannot open health records or reports.
  • We do not sell your information or share it with advertisers.

Your choices

You can update your profile at any time, delete reports you uploaded, and ask for your account to be deactivated through the contact form.

Cookies

We use one cookie to keep you signed in. It cannot be read by scripts on the page, and it is removed when you log out. We do not use advertising cookies.
