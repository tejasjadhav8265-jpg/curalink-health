About CuraLink Health

A demonstration hospital website built to show how booking a doctor can be simple, private and clear.

This is a demo. CuraLink Health is a learning project, not a real hospital. The doctors, prices and reviews are made up, and no real patient data is used.

What you can do here

  • Find a doctor by name, speciality, city, language, fee and more.
  • Book, reschedule or cancel an appointment and get a token number for the day.
  • Keep your reports, prescriptions and visit history in one private place.
  • Ask for an ambulance, book a health package, or share your insurance details.
  • Read short health articles written in plain language.

How we handle your information

Only you and the doctor you have a confirmed appointment with can see your health record and reports. Administrators manage accounts but cannot open medical records. Read more in the privacy policy.
