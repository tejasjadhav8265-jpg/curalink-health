Emergency help

If someone is in danger, call now. Do not wait to fill in a form.

112National emergency number 108Ambulance (most states) 102Mother and child ambulance (many states) 14416Tele-MANAS mental health support (free)
This is a demo website. The numbers above are real Indian emergency numbers, but the hospital, doctors and ambulance on this site are fictional.

Call for help if there is

  • Chest pain or pressure
  • Trouble breathing
  • Face drooping, slurred speech or sudden weakness on one side
  • Heavy bleeding that will not stop
  • Fainting, a seizure or not responding
  • A serious accident, burn or poisoning

While you wait

  1. Stay calm and say exactly where you are.
  2. Keep the person still and warm. Do not give food or drink.
  3. Press firmly on a bleeding wound with a clean cloth.
  4. Unlock the gate or door so the crew can reach you.
